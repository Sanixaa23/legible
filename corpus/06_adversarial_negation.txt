Antibiotics do not work against viruses, and taking them for a cold will not shorten the illness, although it may contribute to resistance in bacteria that are not the target of the treatment at all.

Vaccines rarely cause serious side effects. The risk is not zero, and no medical treatment has a risk of exactly zero, but the rate of serious reactions is far below the rate of serious complications from the diseases themselves.

Correlation does not imply causation. Two measurements may rise together without either causing the other, and in many of the best-known examples neither variable affects the other at all; a third factor affects both.

Mass is not weight. An object's mass does not change when it is moved to the Moon, but its weight does, because weight is the force of gravity acting on that mass and the Moon's gravitational field strength is about one sixth of the Earth's.
